    The Magic

    When her father died in India and his fortune went with him, Sara Crewe stopped being Miss Minchin's show pupil and became her servant. She sleeps now in the attic, under the slates, with the rats.

    You are Sara. It is the coldest night of the winter, and someone has been in your room. Tonight you mean to find out who.
